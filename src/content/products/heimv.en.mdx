<p>
  HeimV is a management tool for Swiss scout and group houses. It supports the entire reservation
  and billing process, from inquiries and bookings to contracts, invoices and payment tracking.
  Custom booking rules, reusable text templates, and website and calendar integrations help make
  day-to-day administration easier.
</p>

<p>
  HeimV is developed and operated in Switzerland, and data is hosted on servers in Switzerland.
  Clubs can self-host the software for their own use, with the source code available on GitHub.
  Hosted plans are available for scout homes and other rental properties.
</p>
